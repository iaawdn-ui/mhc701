## 📤 Export Features

### Export Options

The textbook includes comprehensive export functionality allowing you to save content in multiple formats:

#### 1. **PDF Export**
- **Current Chapter**: Export the chapter you're currently viewing as a PDF
- **Complete Textbook**: Export all 18 chapters plus supplementary materials as a single PDF
- **Features**: Professional formatting, includes all diagrams and tables

#### 2. **DOCX Export (Word)**
- **Current Chapter**: Export as a Microsoft Word document
- **Complete Textbook**: Export entire textbook as a Word document
- **Features**: Editable format, preserves headings and formatting, compatible with Word processors

#### 3. **Print Option**
- **Current Page**: Use browser's native print dialog
- **Features**: Can save as PDF using browser's print-to-PDF functionality, customizable print settings

### How to Export

1. **Navigate** to the chapter or section you want to export
2. **Click** the green "Export" button in the top-right header
3. **Choose** your export option:
   - **Current Chapter**: PDF, DOCX, or Print
   - **Complete Textbook**: Full PDF, Full DOCX, or Print All
4. **Wait** for generation (larger exports may take a moment)
5. **Download** will begin automatically

### Export Tips

- **For best PDF quality**: Use the dedicated PDF export button
- **For editing**: Use DOCX format to modify content in Word
- **For quick reference**: Use the Print option for immediate access
- **For offline reading**: Export complete textbook as PDF
- **For assignments**: Export specific chapters as needed

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. Articulate the history, scope, and professional identity of mental health counseling
2. Establish effective therapeutic relationships with diverse clients
3. Demonstrate core counseling communication and interviewing skills
4. Describe and compare major theoretical orientations in counseling
5. Conduct comprehensive biopsychosocial assessments
6. Develop collaborative, evidence-based treatment plans
7. Apply cultural competence and social justice principles in practice
8. Navigate ethical dilemmas using systematic decision-making models
9. Maintain appropriate clinical documentation
10. Evaluate and apply research evidence to clinical practice
11. Develop a professional identity and career plan
12. Understand the role of supervision in professional development
13. Apply counseling skills to work with diverse and special populations
14. Understand community mental health systems and crisis intervention

## 🐛 Export Troubleshooting

### PDF Export Issues
- **Large file size**: Normal for complete textbook with images
- **Slow generation**: Expected for full textbook; be patient
- **Missing images**: Ensure internet connection for image loading
- **Formatting issues**: Try the Print option as alternative

### DOCX Export Issues
- **File won't open**: Ensure you have Microsoft Word or compatible software
- **Missing formatting**: Some complex layouts may simplify in Word
- **Large file**: Complete textbook DOCX will be substantial

### General Issues
- **Export button not working**: Refresh the page and try again
- **Download not starting**: Check browser download settings
- **Browser compatibility**: Use Chrome or Edge for best results
