# Quick Export Guide

## How to Export Your Textbook

### 📤 Export Button Location
The **Export** button is located in the top-right corner of the header (green button with download icon).

---

## Export Options

### 1️⃣ Current Chapter Export

**What it does:** Exports only the chapter you're currently viewing.

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to the desired chapter
2. Click the green "Export" button
3. Under "Current Chapter" section, choose:
   - **PDF** - Professional PDF format
   - **DOCX** - Microsoft Word format
   - **Print** - Browser print dialog

**Best for:**
- Printing a single chapter
- Sharing specific content with colleagues
- Creating study materials for specific topics
- Quick reference documents

---

### 2️⃣ Complete Textbook Export

**What it does:** Exports all 18 chapters plus supplementary materials as one document.

**Steps:**
1. Click the green "Export" button
2. Under "Complete Textbook" section, choose:
   - **Full PDF** - Entire textbook as PDF
   - **Full DOCX** - Entire textbook as Word document
   - **Print All** - Print entire textbook

**Best for:**
- Offline reading
- Complete reference library
- Printing the entire textbook
- Archiving purposes

**Note:** Full textbook export may take 30-60 seconds to generate.

## Tips for Best Results

### PDF Export
✅ **Use when:** You need perfect formatting and professional appearance  
✅ **Best for:** Sharing, printing, offline reading  
⚠️ **Note:** Large files (especially complete textbook) may take time to generate

### DOCX Export
✅ **Use when:** You need to edit or customize the content  
✅ **Best for:** Creating study guides, taking notes, modifying content  
⚠️ **Note:** Some complex diagrams may appear simplified in Word

### Print Option
✅ **Use when:** You need immediate access or custom print settings  
✅ **Best for:** Quick reference, specific pages, custom formatting  
⚠️ **Note:** Use browser's "Save as PDF" option for PDF output

## Troubleshooting

### Export Not Starting
- Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Clear browser cache

### Export Takes Too Long
- **Normal:** Full textbook export can take 1-2 minutes
- **Solution:** Be patient, don't close the browser
- **Alternative:** Export individual chapters instead

### Download Not Appearing
- Check browser download folder
- Check browser download settings
- Allow pop-ups for this site
- Try a different browser

### File Won't Open
- **PDF:** Use Adobe Acrobat Reader or browser PDF viewer
- **DOCX:** Use Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice
- **Corrupted file:** Try exporting again

## Keyboard Shortcuts

While viewing the textbook:
- **← (Left Arrow)** - Previous chapter
- **→ (Right Arrow)** - Next chapter
- **Ctrl + P** - Print current view
- **Ctrl + S** - Save page (browser default)
